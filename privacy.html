Privacy Policy

Widespread Resources LLC

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This application ("App") is an internal business tool that connects to QuickBooks Online to retrieve and manage financial data for Widespread Resources LLC.

Data We Access

The App accesses QuickBooks Online accounting data (transactions, invoices, bills, account balances) solely for internal business reporting and management purposes.

Data Storage

Financial data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is stored locally on company-controlled devices. Data is cached in a local SQLite database for offline reporting. No data is transmitted to third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or distribute any QuickBooks data to third parties. Data access is limited to authorized personnel of Widespread Resources LLC.

Data Retention

Cached data is retained only as long as needed for business operations and may be deleted at any time.
